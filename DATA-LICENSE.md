Dr Tom's Lexicon is a free, non-commercial, fan-made project. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any show, film, studio, network or author it quotes.

## The word data: CC BY-SA 4.0

The word lists, definitions, etymologies and cross-references (the `.json` files, the word list inside `index.html`, and the spreadsheets in `harvest/`) build on Wiktionary, Wikiquote, wordfreq and the Etymological Wordnet, which are share-alike licensed. So this data is released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
If you reuse it, credit "Dr Tom's Lexicon", the sources below, and share your version under the same licence.

## Quotations

Every quotation from a TV show or film remains the copyright of its owner. The project quotes short lines (15 words at most) only, to study and comment on the words in them, and names the show or film each comes from. Many lines were found through [Wikiquote](https://en.wikiquote.org/). The CC BY-SA licence above does not cover the quotations themselves.

**Removal requests:** if you hold the rights in a quotation and would like it removed, [open an issue](https://github.com/ThomasSchmierer/Moira-Lexicon/issues/new) or use "Report a problem" on the word in the game. It will be taken down.
