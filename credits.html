Credits and licences

Dr Tom's Lexicon is a free, non-commercial word game made by a fan. There are no ads and nothing to buy. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any show, film, studio, network or author it quotes.

Quotations from TV and film

Each word comes with one short line (15 words at most) from the show or film where it was heard. The lines are quoted to study and comment on the words in them, and each one names its show or film and links to where it was found. Copyright in every quotation stays with its owner.

Many lines were found through Wikiquote. Wikiquote's own text is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0; that licence doesn't cover the quotations themselves.

Want a quotation removed? If you hold the rights in a line and would like it taken down, open a removal request, or tap "Report a problem" on the word in the game. It will be removed.
